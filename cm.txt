Fix clipped descender and strengthen the mobile portrait

The "j" in the hero name rendered as an "i" because `background-clip: text`
paints only inside the element's box, and a line-height of 1 left no room
below the baseline, guillotining the descender. Give the line box room and pad
the element, then pull the layout back with a matching negative margin.

On phones the engraving was both too small and too faint to read as a portrait
at all. Raise its opacity and widen the mask core, and scale it up on tall
devices. Short screens keep a smaller crop and a tighter copy rhythm, since a
larger portrait there pushes the calls to action off the page.
